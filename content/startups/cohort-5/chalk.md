Chalk helps coaches game-plan by preparing scouting reports and scouting cards, labeling scout film, and providing a football-native AI assistant that acts as a second brain for coaching staff.

Founded by Aidan Chen (HMC) and Connor Wang (Pomona).

At Chalk, we're building a football coaching platform that brings film, playbooks, scouting, and team communication together to help coaches turn game film into useful insights.
We're looking for driven, passionate individuals with experience or interest in software, design, data, or AI. We're excited to work with people who want to make complex tools genuinely useful for coaches and players.
Your job will be to help shape and build Chalk: designing and developing product features and improving how teams prepare for games. Projects will be scoped to your strengths and may include software development, product design, user research, data analysis, or football research.
- Curious, dependable, and comfortable taking initiative
- Clear communicator who enjoys collaborating across disciplines
- Experience with, or eagerness to learn, software development, UI/UX design, AI/ML, or data analysis
- Able to turn an open-ended problem into practical next steps
- Thoughtful about privacy and the needs of coaches and student athletes
