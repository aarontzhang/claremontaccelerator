Immify brings client intake, document analysis, USCIS form preparation, legal drafting, and case management into one workspace, helping immigration attorneys and their teams spend less time on repetitive paperwork and more time developing strong cases for their clients.

Founded by Harvey Mudd College's Michael Fan.
