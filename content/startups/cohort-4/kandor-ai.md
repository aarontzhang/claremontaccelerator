Kandor streamlines operations for specialty medical clinics across the United States. The platform modernizes how the estimated 50,000 specialty clinics in America function, empowering both physicians and patients at scale.

By replacing outdated workflows with intelligent automation, Kandor gives clinics the tools they need to run more efficiently and focus on delivering better patient care.
