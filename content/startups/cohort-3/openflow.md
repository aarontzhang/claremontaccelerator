Preman is a testing platform for how AI agents interact with your product. As agents choose tools, chain actions, and take paths your team never wrote a test for, Preman helps catch workflow failures before they reach production — simulating agent traffic against your MCP servers from the terminal.

The platform runs in three phases: Simulate spins up a sandbox with production-like data, tools, and business rules described in plain English; Probe runs parallel agent traffic against your MCP servers to test connections, tool discovery, and concurrent behavior; Repeat turns discovered failures into a regression suite you re-run after future changes. It integrates with Cursor, Claude Code, and Codex. Founded by Harvey Mudd College student Alspencer Omondi, who won the 2024 HMC Dean Sundberg Prize.
